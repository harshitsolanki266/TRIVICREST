About us

Indian spices and food products, chosen with care and packed for the world.

Our story

Trivicrest International LLP is an Indian exporter of spices and food products, based in Rajasthan. We started with a simple idea: buyers abroad should receive products that are clean, fresh and consistent, lot after lot.

We source from India’s spice-growing regions and trusted processors, clean and grade every lot, and pack it for export, under your brand or ours.

We keep our range focused so we can do fewer things and do them well. If you need something that isn’t listed, tell us. We’ll tell you honestly whether we can supply it.

What we stand for


Quality first We check every lot before it is packed. Lab testing is available on request.
Clear pricing Clear quotes with no hidden charges.
Reliable supply Consistent stock and clear delivery timelines.
Honest communication Straight answers on specs, samples and shipping, before you commit

How we work

From the farm to your kitchen in four steps.

1. Source We buy from trusted growers and processors.
2. Clean & process Stones, dust and stems are removed.
3. Grind, blend and grade Small batches keep the aroma strong.
4. Pack and ship Sealed, labelled and packed for export.
